1. Introduction

Study model: Al Enezi and Verbrugge (2023)

Question: can players tell dynamic guard patrols apart, in difficulty and in enjoyment?

Design: 115 university students, online, about 15 minutes. Each played three 120 s rounds against three patrol behaviours, in random order, then chose the most enjoyable and the most challenging.

Results: players agreed on the most difficult (χ² = 55.76, p < .001). Enjoyment did not reach significance (χ² = 5.46, p = .06), although the abstract claims both.

What I copy: every player meets every condition, in a balanced order, with a reason given after play.

How this project differs: full sessions of about 25 waves, a measure taken from what players build, and an opponent that learns during play.

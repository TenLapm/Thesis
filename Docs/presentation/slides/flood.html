2. Methodology

Pathfinding: one flood, no scripted choice

Flow field: a weighted flood from the core gives every tile its next step (Bellman, 1958; Moore, 1959; Emerson, 2019). Costs are integers, so no float enters.

Walk round or dig comes from the costs alone: no code makes that choice, and one layout becomes a different problem for each kind of enemy.

Speed: all four fields rebuild in 1.32 ms on the 48 × 48 board. A* for 500 enemies takes 18.2 ms, against 0.14 ms for one field on 38 × 38.

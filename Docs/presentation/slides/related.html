1. Introduction

Our difference

The goal is behaviour. The director is judged by whether the player changes the build, not by how hard the game is or whether the AI wins.

Difficulty is held equal. Every strategy spends the same threat budget, so “it was harder” cannot explain the result. None of the four systems does this.

It learns about the build. A small estimate per type of build and strategy, with a bounded effect, on top of authored scores.

It is tested on people, within subject, by what they actually build.

All planned: only the development director runs today, and it does not learn.

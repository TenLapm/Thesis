1. Introduction

DDA in tower defence (Sutoyo et al., 2015)

Problem: players repeat the same stages against the same enemies, and quit if a stage is far too hard or too easy.

Method: good strategy gets a higher difficulty, bad strategy a lower one. Difficulty depends on the player’s lives, the enemies’ health, and the passive skill points the player chose.

Reported output: the game, a DDA document, and gameplay outputs for best, average and worst strategy cases.

How this project differs: same genre, but it reads what the player built, not how well they are doing. It changes the shape of the wave at equal difficulty, and it is judged by whether people rebuild more.

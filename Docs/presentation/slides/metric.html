2. Methodology

How we measure it: entropy, within each player

H = −Σ p(r) ln p(r) / ln K

The measure: Shannon (1948) entropy of where the player builds, over 6 × 6 regions. 0 is one region; 1 is an even spread.

The comparison: about 24 players each play both versions, on two fixed seed sets, in balanced order. Wilcoxon signed-rank test (Wilcoxon, 1945), p < .05 and dz ≥ 0.5 (Lakens, 2013).

Pass and fail are fixed in advance. Gates G1 to G7 cover the learner, its cost, data per bin, rebuilding, fairness, and whether players notice. A failure names the action to take.

Before any player: offline, random < static < heuristic < full director.

One tester’s 77 waves: 555 tiles, H = 0.62.

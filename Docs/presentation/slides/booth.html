1. Introduction

Left 4 Dead’s AI Director (Booth, 2009)

Goal: dramatic pacing, with peaks and valleys of intensity.

Reads each player: “intensity” rises with damage taken and nearby deaths, and decays over time. The director uses the highest of the four players.

Then cycles: Build Up → Sustain Peak (3 to 5 s) → Peak Fade → Relax (30 to 45 s). Threats are full in the first two phases and minimal in the last two.

What I take from it: a hand-written estimate of the player is enough to drive a director. Layer 2 of this design plays the same role.

How this project differs: it reads how stressed the player is, not what the player built. It changes how much is sent, while here every strategy spends one threat budget.

2. Methodology

Towers and enemies: resolved in whole ticks

Towers. A hit lands in the tick it is fired. There are no projectiles and no dice in the rules, so a replay sees the same fight.

Power. A tower fires only while powered. Assignment is a bipartite matching, so as many towers as possible stay on.

Enemies by movement. Ground enemies follow the field and chew walls. Sappers have a field of their own, where built tiles are cheap. Flyers go straight to the core.

Enemies by target. Raiders go for collectors. Siege enemies knock towers out until the next build phase.

The core’s reach in gold, with a line to each tower it powers.

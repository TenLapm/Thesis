Left 4 Dead’s AI Director (Booth, 2009)

Goal: dramatic pacing, with peaks and valleys of intensity.

Reads each player: “intensity” rises with damage taken, being incapacitated or pulled off a ledge, and nearby deaths. It decays over time, but not while under attack.

Uses the highest of the four players.

PhaseThreatsUntil
Build UpFullIntensity crosses the peak threshold
Sustain PeakFull3 to 5 s after the peak
Peak FadeMinimalIntensity falls and the action breaks
RelaxMinimal30 to 45 s, or far enough toward the safe room

Full: wanderers, mobs, special infected. Minimal: none of these. Boss encounters are not affected.

What I take from it: a hand-written estimate of the player is enough to drive a director. Layer 2 has the same role here, and it must be a complete director on its own.

Where this differs: it reads how stressed the player is, not what the player built. It changes how much is sent, while here every strategy spends one threat budget. The talk describes fixed rules, with no learning.

5

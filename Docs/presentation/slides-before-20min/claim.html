The claim: players rebuild more

Problem. A good player finds one strong layout and repeats it, because fixed waves keep testing the same thing.

Idea. Before each wave, a director reads the player’s build and sends the kind of attack that presses on its weak point. Total enemy strength stays equal to the static wave.

Measured. Shannon entropy of where the player builds, compared within each player: static against adaptive.

Not claimed. That the AI wins more. A harder wave that changes nothing about how the player builds counts as a regression.

The director announces a raid because the player’s collectors have no tower cover. Green line: where raiders will walk.

Leaving a plain wall tile costs 15 × 10 = 150, against 10 on open ground: 14 extra tiles.

A sapper pays 15 × 0.2 = 3, so 18 + 2 = 20. It digs whenever the detour costs more than 2.

An enemy standing on a built tile chews it. When the tile falls, all fields rebuild and every enemy carries on from where it stands.

Reward: behaviour change, credited backwards

r = 0.45·restructure + 0.25·survived + 0.20·pressure + 0.10·novelty

TermMeaningWhy
restructureL1 change in the normalised profile, cappedThis is the behaviour the thesis wants
survivedCore still standing after the waveA defeated player builds nothing more
pressureShare of the wave that came close to the coreA wave nobody feels asks no question
noveltyDistance from last wave’s compositionAvoids sending the same wave again

Control for G3: a binary reward, (restructure ≥ τ) and survived, is also implemented, to test whether graded rewards learn faster.

Design, not yet code. WaveOutcome already records pressure, and telemetry has a reward field waiting.

Study model: Al Enezi and Verbrugge (2023)

Question: can players tell dynamic guard patrols apart, in difficulty and in enjoyment?

Design: 115 university students online, about 15 minutes. Each played three 120 s rounds against VisMesh, RoadMap and Random patrols, in random order, on one fixed map.

Measures: score, then a forced choice of the most enjoyable, challenging and effective team, with a reason in free text.
